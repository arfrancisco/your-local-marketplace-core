The Service is a venue that connects vendors and customers. **We are not

## 6. Orders: lifecycle, rejection, and cancellation

An order moves through a fixed set of states: placed, accepted,
preparing, then either ready for pickup or out for delivery, then
completed. An order may also end as rejected or cancelled. Every change
is an explicit action taken by you or the vendor, is recorded with who
made it and when, and cannot be skipped or reversed. We never infer an
order's state from the content of chat messages.

- **A vendor may reject** an order while it is still in the "placed"
  state, before accepting it.
- **A vendor may cancel** an order in the "placed" or "accepted" state.
- **A customer may cancel** an order in the "placed" or "accepted" state,
  and may not make any other status change.
- **Once an order is being prepared, it can no longer be cancelled in the
  app** by either side. If something goes wrong after that point, work it
  out with the other party in the order's chat.
- Completed, rejected, and cancelled orders are final states. There is no
  in-app refund, reversal, or order-edit mechanism, because the app never
  handles the money in the first place (Section 7).

Because payment happens off-platform, cancelling an order in the app does
not move any money. If a customer has already paid a vendor for an order
that is later cancelled, arranging the refund is entirely between the
customer and the vendor.

## 8. Chat

Each order has its own chat between the customer and the vendor on that
order. Chat is for communicating about that order: arranging pickup or
delivery, sharing proof of payment, asking questions. Messages are
delivered in real time and are visible only to the two participants in
that order.

The vendor's payment instructions and QR code are shown as a pinned panel
above the chat rather than as a chat message. That panel is read live from
the vendor's current shop settings, so if a vendor updates their payment
details, the panel updates for all of their orders, including past ones.

Chat is not read or parsed by the app to make decisions. Order status only
ever changes through explicit actions, never by us interpreting what you
typed. Messages, including any image you attach, are a permanent record
tied to the order and visible to the other party for as long as the order
data is retained (see the Privacy Policy). There is no message editing or
deletion in the app.

Do not use chat to harass, threaten, or share content unrelated to the
order. We may remove access to chat, or suspend an account, for abuse.
